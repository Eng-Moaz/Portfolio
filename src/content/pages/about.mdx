## Modaresy, my first startup

Modaresy (مدرّسي) was a marketplace connecting students with tutors, and my first experience building a startup with a team. My role was CAIO, focused on AI, but much of what I took from it happened beyond the technical work.

We went through extensive business training and developed our business model, learning what it takes to turn an idea into a viable business. I also spoke with hundreds of teachers. Those conversations taught me about communicating, understanding people’s needs, and working with different personalities. We eventually won third place at the Creativa Ismailia competition; what stays with me is how the experience opened up the business and human sides of building a product.

## Linux, Neovim, and making the tools mine

I enjoy Linux and Neovim partly because they invite curiosity. My development environment is something I can open up, understand, and shape around the way I work. Adjusting a keybinding, reading a configuration file, or tracing why a tool behaves a certain way is not separate from programming for me. It is another form of building.

That setup will keep changing, which is exactly why I want to leave room for it here rather than freeze it into a list of technologies.
